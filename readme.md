## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **AI Engineer & Full-Stack Developer** operating at the intersection of machine intelligence, robust engineering, and human-centered design. I design, build, and ship end-to-end AI-powered products from concept to high-availability production environments.

* 🧠 **AI & Automation:** Claude Code Specialist, Prompt Engineering, Autonomous AI Agents, MCP, Ollama, & LLM Workflows.
* 💻 **Full-Stack Core:** Modern web application architectures with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Python, FastAPI, and Node.js.
* 🎨 **UI/UX & Systems:** Tokenized Design Systems, Presentation Automation, & High-Fidelity Interactive Interfaces.
